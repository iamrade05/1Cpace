How the board is calculated & package breakdown

Sales count when compliance first becomes complete, using South African dates and the credited consultant. Current compliance blockers exclude credit. Daily pace is the remaining balance divided by remaining planning days, rounded up to one decimal. These Monday–Saturday planning days are estimates, not staff attendance or a holiday-adjusted roster. A dash means no target or no remaining days. Commitments are planned units for the board day.

Package names come from credited applications; categories are not inferred from the photograph.
